Email 1: Creating Your Money Ritual

Subject: Day 1: Create Your Money Ritual

Primary action: Join the live training and complete the ritual.

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Welcome to Day One of our Money Manifestation Session! I'm so happy you're here.

Today, we're going to create a money ritual of your own.

What is a ritual, and why do we practice one? A ritual is a habit or activity we use to honor and strengthen our relationship with a person, belief, or feeling. If you know me as a teacher, you know I'm all about FEELINGS. What we feel inside influences what we experience in the world around us.

That's what today's exercise is about: changing how we FEEL about money so we can open ourselves to a greater flow of wealth and abundance.

Step One: Create your statement of intention.

Start with any of the statements below. Choose the words that resonate with you, combine a few, or write something completely your own. Your intention can grow and change throughout this session and beyond.

  • I, [name], humbly open myself to all aligned sources of wealth that were previously blocked for me.
  • I, [name], call upon the highest divinity to open me to endless abundance.
  • I, [name], make space within myself to bring in cash flow now!
  • I, [name], call [amount] into my personal funds so I can live in alignment with my true purpose.
  • I, [name], affirm that all abundance coming to me will help me live my purpose, find joy, and uplift the world.
  • I, [name], open myself to fully seeing the abundance that is my birthright and that I can access now.

For today's task, *|FNAME|*, put together a statement that FEELS good to you. For an extra touch of ceremony, write it by hand and decorate the page. Make it personal!

Step Two: Make your favorite drink.

Prepare tea, coffee, or simply a glass of water. Hold the cup in your hands, taking care if it's hot. Pause and feel gratitude for what you already have, and for the wealth and abundance that are already on their way to you.

When you feel ready, speak your intention aloud or quietly to yourself.

Repeat this ritual as often as you like today. Change it to suit your needs. Most importantly, have fun with it and FEEL.

Want to explore rituals together? Join today's Facebook Live in the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe at 2 p.m. Eastern. I'll walk through the exercise and answer your questions.

Until tomorrow!

Yours in abundance, Rachel

Email 2: Acknowledging Your Money Beliefs

Subject: Day 2: What's Blocking Your Sense of Abundance?

Primary action: Complete a private journaling exercise.

Hi *|FNAME|*,

How are you feeling since yesterday? I hope you enjoyed creating your money ritual. Keep practicing it if it feels good to you.

Missed Day One? Revisit the Money Ritual email.

Today's work is about acknowledgment. Before we can change how we think and feel about money, we need to notice the beliefs we're carrying. Some may be obvious. Others may have been with us for so long that we barely notice them.

Get out your journal and favorite pen. Set aside about 20 minutes if you can, but any amount of time is fine. Make yourself comfortable. Put on music, wrap up in a blanket, or make a cup of tea. Create a space where you feel safe and supported.

This is a two-day process. Today we'll begin with two exercises. Do what feels manageable for you.

You can start with this intention, or write your own:

*I, *|FNAME|*, open a safe space to express pent-up emotions. I intend to release and archive these feelings so I can bring in new opportunities, experiences, and abundance. I am grateful for the freedom to change my emotions and my life.*

Part One: List your beliefs about money.

Write down any belief that feels heavy, uncomfortable, or limiting. Your list might include statements like these:

  • I have to work hard for every dollar.
  • Money is difficult to make.
  • People who have a lot of money are bad.
  • Having money could put me in danger.
  • I don't understand how money works.

You might write ten items or fifty. Don't censor yourself. A belief still belongs on the page even if you only feel it sometimes.

There is no need to judge what you write. The purpose is simply to notice it.

Part Two (optional): Release emotions connected to an experience.

If a person or event comes to mind, write privately about the feelings connected to it. That could include anger, sadness, regret, or embarrassment. Let the pent-up emotions flow onto the page. You can write a letter to the person or situation that caused you pain. This letter is for you alone, and you will not send it. Giving those feelings a voice helps release what you've been carrying.

You are in charge of this exercise. You can pause, skip it, or return another day if you need more time.

Be gentle with yourself. This kind of release can bring up tears, anger, anxiety, or other powerful feelings as pent-up emotions begin to move. Honor your body, give yourself room to feel what comes up, and know that this is part of the releasing process.

Join today's Facebook Live in the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe at 4 p.m. Eastern if you have questions about the exercise. You can also reply to this email and tell me how it's going.

Sending you love and encouragement, Rachel

Email 3: Rewriting Your Money Story

Subject: Day 3: Turn Your Money Beliefs Around

Primary action: Reframe the statements from Day Two.

Hi, dearest *|FNAME|*,

How did yesterday's journaling go? I know reflecting on old beliefs can bring up strong feelings. Thank you for giving yourself the time and care to do it.

Need a refresher? Revisit Day One's Money Ritual or Day Two's journaling exercise.

Today we're going to take the list you made and transform those old beliefs into a new program for your brain and heart. If you haven't finished yesterday's exercise, complete it before moving on.

GET EXCITED! WE'RE MAKING ROOM FOR NEW THOUGHTS AND FEELINGS!

Take out yesterday's list, a fresh sheet of paper, and a pen.

Begin with this intention, or make it your own:

*I, *|FNAME|*, intend to release the beliefs about money that no longer serve me. I am ready to be healed in my abundance. I welcome new beliefs that will bring me into prosperity. I ask for divine guidance to move through me and bring these new beliefs to me now. Thank you.*

Step One: Write the opposite of each old belief.

For every limiting statement on yesterday's list, write its opposite on a fresh page. Make the new statements bold, exciting, and personal. For example:

  • Old: Money is difficult to make. New: Money is EASY to make!
  • Old: I have to work hard for every dollar. New: Cash flows to me without my even trying.
  • Old: If I have money, I will be in danger. New: I am completely safe AND really rich.
  • Old: I don't understand how money works. New: I am an expert in my money.

Make the list your own, and address every belief you wrote down yesterday. These are your new truths about money and abundance.

Step Two: Keep the new list close.

Photograph it, make a copy, or tuck it into your bag. When an old thought returns, take a look at the new statement and ask yourself:

*What is it like when [my new statement] is my reality?*

You can also practice with your new list when you're feeling calm and positive. This is your new programming for your brain and heart. The more often you repeat it, the more these new thoughts become your automatic response. Your powerful intention is already breaking through the old programming. It may take time, but trust that the work is taking effect. Results are on their way!

Part Two (optional): Consider a different perspective.

If you wrote about a painful experience yesterday, list the silver linings or strengths that emerged from it. You might write, "I learned that I can survive difficult experiences," or "Now I know what to avoid in the future." Then begin writing forgiveness statements for the person or situation that has been holding you back.

If you aren't ready to forgive, that's okay. Return to the unsent letters and release your feelings for as many rounds as you need. As you continue, the emotional charge will lessen, helping you let go of what has kept you stuck in your money flow.

Join today's Facebook Live in the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe at 4 p.m. Eastern to ask questions and share what you're discovering.

You're doing beautiful work. Keep going!

Yours in abundance and prosperity, Rachel

Email 4: Making Room for Vision

Subject: Day 4: Picture Your Life with Abundance

Primary action: Complete a guided visualization.

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Welcome to Day Four of our Money Manifestation Session! Can you believe how much we've explored in just a few days?

We've practiced a money ritual, released limiting beliefs, and begun reprogramming our brains and hearts to welcome abundance. Now let's build on that foundation by allowing ourselves to dream.

Want to revisit an exercise? Return to Day One's ritual, Day Two's journaling, or Day Three's reframing practice.

Today is about VISION. There are many ways to visualize what you want, but this is one of my favorites. I hope you'll have fun with it.

Begin with an intention, or create one of your own:

*With an open heart and mind, I, *|FNAME|*, create a sacred space for vision. I call on divine guidance to move through me as I welcome a clear vision of abundance into my heart and mind. I intend to bring in wealth from aligned, sustainable sources so I can bring myself more fully into the world.*

Here is your visualization exercise.

Imagine that money could become a person, just as we sometimes imagine nature as Mother Nature.

What would this person look like? How would they dress? What would their voice sound like? There is no right or wrong answer. Let the image arrive in whatever way feels natural to you.

Now imagine that this person is a trusted friend, or even a loving partner. You're spending a wonderful day together, doing some of the things you've always dreamed of doing.

Perhaps you're traveling to a place on your bucket list, going on a shopping spree, spending a night on the town, or contributing to a cause that matters to you. Each time a bill arrives, Money jumps up and pays it with ease. Money looks at you and says, "I love you! I am endless abundance, and I'll always be here for you."

Notice where you are, who is with you, and how you feel. Experience what it's like to be completely aligned with abundance and to know that Money is always there for you.

Stay with that feeling for a few moments. Let yourself believe that this abundance is already available to you.

Practice this visualization whenever it inspires you. Make it vivid, make it yours, and above all, have fun!

Do you love this exercise as much as I do? Reply and tell me about it. If visualization doesn't come easily, you can reply about that, too. I'd love to hear from you.

I'll also be answering questions at 4 p.m. Eastern in the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe.

Email 5: Taking Inspired Action

Subject: Day 5: Turn Your Money Vision into Action

Primary action: Choose a practical next step and book a complimentary consultation.

Welcome back, sacred money manifestor!

It's the final day of our five-day session. Are you ready for the step that brings everything together?

Taking inspired action!

We've spent the last four days exploring intention, emotion, beliefs, and vision. Today, we're going to add the missing ingredient that brings manifestation into the physical world.

I like to describe my approach with a simple formula I call Magic 101:

(Intention or Desire + Strong Emotion) x Faith + Action = Result

With this formula, you can manifest ANYTHING you want to bring into your universe. Here's how it works:

Intention: Decide exactly what you want the universe to bring to you. On Day One, you began putting that desire into words.

Strong Emotion: Any powerful emotion can fuel manifestation, whether it's fear, anxiety, anger, love, happiness, joy, or confidence. Remember that the feeling you put into your intention shapes what comes back to you. Manifest through anger, and you invite more anger. Manifest through love and joy, and you invite more of those feelings into your life.

Faith: There can be a delay between giving your instruction to the universe and seeing the result in your life. Faith bridges that gap. It keeps the channel open until your manifestation arrives.

Action: This is where the rubber meets the road! Inspired action moves possibilities into the physical world and shows the universe that you are ready to take part in the co-creation process.

The order doesn't have to be exact. You may begin with action and come to faith or intention later. What matters is adding ACTION to the powerful inner work you've already completed.

Today's assignment: Choose one inspired action.

Your step doesn't have to be enormous. It could be:

  • Checking a credit report you've been avoiding.
  • Opening a savings account with a small deposit.
  • Putting together a simple spending plan.
  • Looking for a job opportunity that fits your skills.
  • Having an honest conversation with someone you trust about your financial goals.

Choose something specific and manageable, but make it BOLD and INSPIRED. Every action sends another signal to the universe that you're ready to receive abundance.
