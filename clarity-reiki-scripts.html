Video 1: Welcome to the Empath's Protection Toolkit

Hello, and welcome to the Empath's Protection Toolkit. My name is Rachel Haymer. I'm the founder of Clarity Reiki and the creator of this toolkit, and I'm so thrilled to welcome you and be your guide on this journey.

I created these practices to help you feel safe, supported, and connected to yourself as you move through the world. The idea is to give you tools you can return to whenever you feel the need to cleanse your energy, renew your sense of protection, or simply take a moment to come back into alignment.

There are six processes in the toolkit, divided into two sections. Section One focuses on protection and cleansing. It also introduces ways to notice when you feel affected by energy that seems to come from outside yourself, and whether you want to spend more time on a cleansing practice.

Section Two is designed especially for empaths who work closely with other people's energy. That may include tarot readers, energy readers, healers, or anyone whose work involves sensitive, direct interaction with others. These are more advanced practices, and they build on the foundation of Section One.

Whatever your level of experience, I encourage you to become familiar with the first section. If you've already practiced the first track, which is available through Clarity Reiki's free gift, you can move on to the next process. Otherwise, give yourself time to learn the basics and notice which exercises feel most natural to you.

As you work through the toolkit, check in with yourself. These visualizations are meant to help you feel protected, happy, and aligned. There is no need to rush. You can revisit a track as often as you like.

I also invite you to join our Facebook community, the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe. It's a small, supportive group that I moderate personally. If you have questions or want to share what you're experiencing, please come in and connect with us. I love hearing from people as they make these practices their own.

Thank you for being here, and thank you for investing your time and energy in yourself. My hope is that these tools will travel with you, whether you're at home, on a crowded New York City street, or anywhere life takes you. I'm so excited to begin this journey together.

Video 2: Process One, Connecting Above and Below

Welcome back to the Empath's Protection Toolkit. I'm Rachel Haymer, and I'm excited to introduce our first process. This is the foundation for the other practices in the toolkit and one of the most important ideas in my approach to protection.

Before we begin, I want to clarify what this exercise is designed for. Sometimes, you may feel as though you've picked up an unfamiliar energy from an unexpected encounter. Maybe it happened on the train, in a crowded room, or during an interaction that left you feeling unsettled. I sometimes think of that feeling as an empath's version of catching a cold.

The purpose of this practice is to help you release what feels unfamiliar and reconnect with yourself. It is not designed to remove someone you've consciously invited into your life, such as a partner, family member, or another important relationship. Working through those deeper connections is a different process. If that's what you're dealing with, you may need more sustained personal support.

Now, let's talk about connecting above and below. In this visualization, we draw from two sources. The first is above us. You may call it God, the universe, Source, a higher vibration, or the divine heart. Use whichever language speaks to you. The second source is below us, in Mother Gaia and the grounding presence of the earth.

Imagine connecting with the energy above and the energy below, then bringing those two sources together within yourself. In my approach, this creates a sense of support and protection without asking you to draw entirely from your own reserves. Instead of exhausting yourself, you can picture an ongoing connection to something larger.

I recommend listening to this track for the first few practices in a place where you feel safe and comfortable. Follow the visualization and become familiar with the feeling of drawing from above and below. Later, you'll hear these ideas again in other processes.

With practice, you may discover that you no longer need the full recording every time. You can remember the imagery and return to that sense of connection whenever you need it. The recording will always be here if you want a refresher.

If any part of the exercise is unclear, come to the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe on Facebook and ask me about it. I'm there to help you work with these tools. For now, settle in, listen to the track, and take your time connecting with your sources.

Video 3: Process Two, Money Showers

Welcome back to the Empath's Protection Toolkit. We are now moving into our second process in Section One, a visualization I call Money Showers.

I love the imagery in this practice. Picture a beautiful shower of light moving down around you, washing through your energy and leaving you with a renewed feeling of clarity. As the visualization continues, imagine that light forming a sphere of protection around you.

Some of you may prefer the first process, where you connect with the energy above and below and imagine a protective field surrounding your body. Others may respond more strongly to this one, where light flows downward like a cleansing shower. Both are intended to bring you back to a feeling of protection and alignment.

You'll notice that the opening of this track is similar to the first. That repetition is intentional. I want the beginning to feel familiar so you can settle into the practice more easily each time. As you learn the sequence, you may find yourself relaxing into it before the main visualization even begins.

I also want to remind you that the most important part is not whether you can form a perfect picture in your mind. Imagery can be fun and powerful, but don't worry if every detail isn't clear. Pay attention to how the practice makes you feel. Do you feel calmer, more centered, or more at ease? That inner experience is what I encourage you to explore.

As you try these first processes, notice which one resonates with you. One may help you feel a stronger connection to your sources, while another helps you imagine a cleansing light. You may enjoy both, and that's wonderful too.

Take your time with Money Showers, and please share your experience with us in the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe. I look forward to hearing which visualizations work best for you. Enjoy the practice, and I'll see you in the next lesson.

Video 4: Process Three, The Empath's Poem

Hello, and welcome back to the Empath's Protection Toolkit. I'm Rachel Haymer, and this is our third process in Section One: the Empath's Poem.

This exercise is a little different from the others because it combines an audio track with a printable PDF. I wanted to give you something you could practice at home and then carry with you into everyday life.

There are moments when it may be hard to tell whether a heavy feeling comes from within you or whether it seems connected to an experience outside yourself. The Empath's Poem is a way to pause, reflect, and ask what you're feeling to become clearer to you.

Here's how I suggest using it. Begin by listening to the audio track in a comfortable place. Print the accompanying PDF if you can. When the spoken words appear in the recording, follow along on the page. You may even want to say them aloud with me during your first few sessions. The aim is to make the words familiar and give you an anchor for the practice.

Once you know the exercise, you can fold the page and keep it in your pocket, purse, or wallet. You can also save a screenshot on your phone. That way, the words are available if you want to return to the visualization while you're out in the world, perhaps on a train, at a festival, or in another busy place.

Please practice at home before trying to use the poem in the middle of a demanding situation. Familiarity will make it easier to return to the exercise when you need it.

You may notice themes that overlap with the first two practices. That's intentional. All three processes focus on cleansing and protection, but they approach those ideas through different images and actions. Some people may find one favorite practice. Others may want to use all three depending on how they feel that day.

If you have questions, bring them to the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe. Tell us what you're noticing and which process speaks to you. When you feel comfortable using at least one of the Section One exercises, you may be ready to explore the more advanced work in Section Two, especially if you regularly work with other people's energy.

Thank you for continuing through the toolkit with me. I hope you're enjoying the experience, and I can't wait to hear how the Empath's Poem works for you.

Video 5: Process Four, Clearing Waves

Hello, and welcome back to the Empath's Protection Toolkit. I'm Rachel Haymer, and today we're exploring a process called Clearing Waves.

I'm especially grateful for this practice because it has become an important part of how I prepare for and recover from energy readings. Over the years, I've worked with thousands of people, and I've found that taking a moment between sessions helps me return to myself before meeting the next client.

If you do readings, healing work, or another type of one-on-one service, you may find this visualization useful as a personal reset. The idea may also appeal to teachers, massage therapists, or anyone who spends much of the day working closely with others. For me, it is particularly meaningful as an energy worker.

We'll begin by drawing from above and below, just as we practiced in Process One. If that connection doesn't yet feel familiar, I encourage you to revisit the first track before continuing.

Next, imagine a pool or ocean of energy forming around you. When you're ready, picture three waves rising and moving through your field. Count them as they come: one, two, three. With each wave, imagine releasing the impressions and feelings you no longer want to carry into your next interaction.

I love how immediate this practice can feel. When I use it, I sometimes notice a physical sense of release, including a feeling of openness around my face and sinuses. That's my personal experience with the visualization, and yours may be different. What matters is finding a way to pause, breathe, and reconnect with yourself.

If you're doing several readings in a row, I encourage you to try this between clients. Give yourself a moment to reset before you begin again.

Clearing Waves is one of the processes I return to most often, and I hope you'll enjoy exploring it. If you need help, come to the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe and ask your questions. When you're ready, continue to our final process, Supernova.

Video 6: Final Process, Supernova

Hello, and welcome back to our final lesson in the Empath's Protection Toolkit. I hope you've enjoyed exploring these practices as much as I've enjoyed sharing them with you.

Our final process is called Supernova. I designed it for times when you've tried the gentler visualizations in Section One and still feel a lingering heaviness or residue. This is a more direct, intention-focused exercise for imagining a complete clearing of your energy field.

Before you begin, remember something I've mentioned throughout the toolkit. These practices are not meant to remove an actual person from your life or resolve a difficult relationship. If your concern is tied to someone you know and have an ongoing connection with, the situation may call for a different kind of work and support.

Supernova is intended for the unfamiliar feelings you want to release from your own space. I recommend beginning with a gentler process, such as Connecting Above and Below or Money Showers, before turning to this more intense visualization.

As always, start by drawing from above and below. Return to the connection you've been building throughout the toolkit. Then follow the track and use your intention to imagine clearing what feels unwelcome. You don't need to force an image or make the experience look a particular way. Focus on the sense of release and on returning to yourself.

One of my hopes for this entire toolkit is that the practices will eventually become familiar enough to use without a recording. You can always return to an audio track when you want guidance or a refresher. Over time, though, you may find it easier to call up the visualization on your own, wherever you happen to be.

I would love to hear how Supernova works for you and which of the six processes you feel most connected to. Please visit the Clarity Reiki Healing Tribe on Facebook to share your experience, ask questions, or connect with others who are practicing alongside you. You can also reach me through Clarity Reiki if you'd like to discuss additional support.

Thank you for joining me on this journey. I hope these exercises help you feel more centered, supported, and free to be your true empathetic self. Be well, and may you move forward feeling protected and safe. Love and light on all your journeys.

Editorial and Source Note

Original project: Clarity Reiki, Empath's Protection Toolkit, developed during Raj Dutt Sharma's 2019-2021 writing and editorial engagement.

Contribution: Transcribed Rachel Haymer's spoken recordings; wrote, rewrote, and edited scripts, including substantial line edits; and organized instructional material for clarity, lesson continuity, pacing, and voice.

Content approach: The six video sections retain the instructor's first-person spiritual framework, warm delivery, and step-by-step teaching style.

Editorial principles: Reduced repetition and run-on sentences; clarified instructions and transitions; kept the instructor's warmth and invitations to the community; distinguished subjective visualization experiences from factual medical claims; and maintained consistent process names and headings.
