Preface

I’ve written eighty-seven poems about life, sobriety, and recovery.

Some programs have twelve steps; I had eighty-seven.

If I can offer anything to anyone who suffers or loves someone who does, it’s this:

Rock Bottom reinvents itself. Pushing toward it only drags you further down.

Enough people probably love you as much as you hate yourself; some of them may even love it when you stop, I can’t say, but what I can say is I’ve found that to stay clean, you need to love yourself a whole lot too, or at least become tolerable.

And I spent 13 years trying and failing to stay clean.

Really annoyed I can’t put that on a resume, anyway.

The denial and the lying, second only to the addiction, are what kill you.

And there’s help out there, which is unfortunate for those who still want to keep getting away with it.

You’ll have enough material for a book if you get clean, though. So there’s that.

Best of luck, and lots of love, if you or anyone you know suffers from it.

Now enjoy the show.
