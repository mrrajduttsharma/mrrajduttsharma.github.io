Anniversary Blog Campaign | August 2017

On August 1, 2011, Vimeo proudly announced Vimeo PRO, a service designed with small businesses, organizations, and serious creators in mind. The goal was to help them make better use of the Vimeo platform for video hosting, audience engagement, and sharing their work.

As the service has grown, so have the creative possibilities available to our users. To celebrate six years of Vimeo PRO, here are six reasons we're looking forward to what's next.

1. 360-Degree Video

With the arrival of VR headsets and 360-degree video, the ways audiences experience creative content are changing. Vimeo's 360 video tools give creators more ways to invite viewers into their work. With support for uploads up to 8K and both monoscopic and stereoscopic formats, creators have new options for presenting immersive video.

2. Portfolios

Showcasing and curating work for specific clients has not always been easy for video creators online. Vimeo PRO's Portfolio feature helps solve that problem by letting creators customize how and what prospective clients see when they view their work.

3. More Bandwidth and Storage

Sometimes creators need to upload a steady stream of high-quality work, whether daily or weekly. Vimeo PRO offers professional creators more room to store and share their videos, without having to build a separate video-hosting solution from scratch.

4. Mobile Viewing

More people are moving away from desktops and laptops to watch video on phones and other mobile devices. For creators, quality matters regardless of screen size. Vimeo PRO helps professionals share videos that viewers can access across devices, while maintaining a high-quality presentation.

5. Customer Service

At Vimeo PRO, we take pride in helping creators get the most out of their accounts. Customer support and feedback from our community help us respond to questions, understand what users need, and continue improving the service.

6. Security and Privacy

Protecting video content online matters more than ever. Vimeo PRO gives creators options for controlling where their videos can be embedded, who can view or download them, and whether access requires a password. These controls help professionals share work with the intended audience.

Social Media Campaign

Supporting Posts and Creative Direction | August 2017

Facebook Post 1

What steps has Vimeo PRO taken to reach its sixth year? Discover six reasons we're celebrating and see how video creators can get more out of the platform.

Visual direction: An excited person wearing a VR headset against a Vimeo-blue background. The image should suggest a forward-looking platform and connect with the 360-degree video feature.

Facebook Post 2

Six reasons Vimeo PRO continues to help creatives and businesses share their work. Take a look at the tools behind the anniversary.

Visual direction: A group of young creatives gathered around a video camera against a clean white background. The image should convey collaboration, community, and a serious approach to filmmaking.

Twitter Post 1

Never heard of Vimeo PRO? We've been here for six years. Here are six reasons to take a look.

Visual direction: A person viewing the Vimeo PRO signup page on a mobile phone. The playful approach should create curiosity while reinforcing mobile access.

Twitter Post 2

Six years of Vimeo PRO. Six reasons to help your #creative #content grow. Read our anniversary roundup.

Visual direction: A young creator holding a camera and smiling against a blue background. The photograph should suggest possibility and encourage readers to visit the blog post.

Instagram Post

We're celebrating six years of Vimeo PRO, six years of creative work, and the tools that help make it possible. Explore six features that help creators share their videos with the world.

Visual direction: A carousel featuring a 360-degree video headset, a Portfolio interface, high-quality video on a mobile device, and a customer support team. Each image should correspond to a benefit covered in the blog post.
